### Design Decisions

#### Seed Entries as the Recovery Vehicle for Squash-Token-Drop
**Decision**: Restore the missing log rows via curated `log.seed.md` entries carrying their own authored date (2026-08-10), 1lah's `.status.yaml` summary verbatim, and the bare `(1lah)` id.
**Why**: Seed entries land under the correct original date, require no artificial file touches, and `mergeSeedEntries` byte-equal dedup keeps every re-run a no-op (FKF §6, Constitution III); the generator remains the sole writer of `log.md` (FKF §5).
